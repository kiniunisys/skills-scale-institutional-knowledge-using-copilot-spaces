# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
The Quality Assurance Lead owns the quality strategy and execution of projects. They define testing approaches, establish quality metrics, and coordinate QA activities to ensure deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and implement testing strategy and test plans
- Oversee quality metrics and defect management
- Coordinate QA activities and testing cycles
- Participate in requirements review to identify testability gaps
- Block releases when critical issues remain unresolved
- Collaborate with developers on test automation and quality improvements
- Report quality status and risks to Project Manager and stakeholders

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal production defects
- Reduce time spent on rework and post-release fixes
- Establish sustainable quality practices and metrics
- Enable fast feedback loops between development and QA

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality status reports
- Defect tracking and escalation logs
- Coordination with Development Lead on testing strategy
- Regular updates to Program Manager on quality metrics

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works closely with Developers** on test planning, test automation, and defect resolution
- **Reports to Project Manager** on quality metrics and release readiness
- **Partners with Product Managers** on acceptance criteria clarity and user scenario testing

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Architect designs technical solutions, ensures architectural consistency across the project, and provides guidance on major technical decisions. They bridge business requirements with technical implementation and help mitigate technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Design and review overall technical architecture and solutions
- Ensure consistency with existing systems and architectural patterns
- Review and approve major technical design decisions and proposals
- Advise on technology choices, trade-offs, and technical risks
- Provide technical guidance to the Development team
- Collaborate on estimating technical complexity and feasibility
- Document architectural decisions and rationale

### Goals
- Enable scalable, maintainable, and reliable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Accelerate development through clear architectural guidance
- Ensure alignment with long-term technical vision

### Typical Communication
- Architectural design documents and decision records
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Guidance on technology choices and implementation approaches
- Risk assessments on technical complexity

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Partners with Developers** on implementation and technical problem-solving
- **Advises Project Manager** on technical risks and feasibility assessments
- **Collaborates with Product Managers** to understand business requirements and technical implications
- **Works with Quality Assurance Lead** on testability and quality requirements

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder Liaison maintains engagement with external and internal stakeholders, gathers requirements and feedback, and communicates project status. They serve as the primary communication bridge between the project team and broader stakeholder community.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain regular communication with key stakeholders
- Gather requirements, feedback, and concerns from stakeholders
- Communicate project status, milestones, and changes to stakeholders
- Escalate stakeholder concerns and critical feedback to Project Manager
- Coordinate stakeholder reviews and sign-offs
- Document stakeholder requirements and expectations
- Facilitate alignment between stakeholder needs and project delivery

### Goals
- Ensure stakeholder alignment and satisfaction throughout the project
- Identify and address stakeholder concerns early
- Maintain transparency and trust with external partners
- Reduce scope creep and miscommunication

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and status reports
- Requirements documentation and feedback logs
- Regular stakeholder meetings and reviews
- Escalation and issue resolution communications

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Reports to Project Manager** with stakeholder updates and concerns
- **Coordinates with Product Managers** on requirement validation and prioritization
- **Works with Developers** to communicate feasibility and technical constraints to stakeholders
- **Supports Quality Assurance Lead** in gathering stakeholder acceptance criteria

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
The Release Manager coordinates all release activities, manages deployment schedules, and tracks the health of deployments. They ensure releases are well-planned, executed smoothly, and monitored effectively.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain release plans and deployment schedules
- Coordinate release readiness across all teams (development, QA, operations)
- Manage release documentation and deployment procedures
- Track deployment progress and resolve deployment issues
- Communicate release status and rollout plans to stakeholders
- Coordinate rollback procedures if issues arise
- Maintain release calendars and manage deployment windows

### Goals
- Execute smooth, predictable releases with minimal disruption
- Reduce time-to-market for new features
- Minimize production incidents related to deployments
- Maintain clear visibility of release status across teams

### Typical Communication
- Release plans and deployment schedules
- Release notes and deployment procedures
- Status updates during deployment windows
- Post-deployment monitoring and incident reports

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works with Quality Assurance Lead** to ensure release readiness and sign-off
- **Coordinates with Developers** on feature completion and deployment readiness
- **Partners with Project Manager** on release scheduling and stakeholder communication
- **Engages Operations/DevOps teams** on deployment execution and infrastructure support

---

## Risk and Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
The Risk and Compliance Officer identifies, tracks, and mitigates project risks while ensuring compliance with organizational policies, regulatory requirements, and governance standards. They provide proactive risk management and escalation support.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and document project risks and compliance requirements
- Maintain risk registers and track mitigation progress
- Assess impact and likelihood of identified risks
- Recommend and implement risk mitigation strategies
- Escalate blocking risks and compliance issues to Project Manager and stakeholders
- Ensure project compliance with organizational policies and regulatory requirements
- Conduct compliance reviews and audits
- Provide risk management guidance and best practices

### Goals
- Minimize project disruptions from unforeseen risks
- Ensure regulatory and policy compliance throughout delivery
- Enable proactive decision-making based on risk awareness
- Build organizational confidence in project execution

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and risk assessment reports
- Compliance checklists and audit findings
- Escalation notifications and mitigation plans
- Risk briefings in project status meetings

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Advises Project Manager and Program Manager** on risk status and escalations
- **Coordinates with Technical Architect** on technical and architectural risks
- **Works with Quality Assurance Lead** on quality and release risks
- **Engages Stakeholder Liaison** on compliance-related communications and regulatory concerns
